# Stream Autopilot

A Windows app that sets up and runs your YouTube / Twitch stream for you: one-click OBS setup, animated overlays,
thumbnails, trending titles, a chat bot and more. Made for streamers in India.

## Download

**[⬇ Download the latest version](https://github.com/charliehere/stream-autopilot/releases/latest)**: get `StreamAutopilot-Setup-x.y.z.exe` under *Assets* and run it.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't code-signed: click **More info → Run anyway**.
After installing, the app updates itself: when a new version is out, it asks you and installs in one click.

## What it does

- **Go Live setup**: best OBS settings for your PC and internet, and all your scenes built in one click
- **Overlays**: animated alerts, chat, polls, UPI QR
- **Thumbnails & titles**: designs and titles based on what's trending right now
- **Chat bot**: points, polls, viewer queue, !recap, read-out-loud Super Chats and UPI tips
- **Audio, camera, music and Shorts** tools

## Pro

Free to use. **Pro** adds Studio (run the stream from the app), the phone remote, stream recaps, going-live posts, predictions,
the stream guard, automations, the thumbnail designer, Auto-Shorts, overlay themes & widgets, vertical live and the encoder test.
Every install includes a **7-day free trial**. After that, Pro is from **₹199/month**: open the **Pro** tab in the app,
pay by UPI and send the proof from there to get your activation code. A **PRO** badge shows next to the name once it's active.

Works with OBS Studio (everything) and Streamlabs Desktop (most features).
